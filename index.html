Volcanoes — Mantle Melting & Subduction

Explore how subduction and mantle melting create volcanoes.

This piece focuses on a volcanic mountain built by subduction-driven mantle melting, and also includes a river meander scene to show how erosion reshapes the land. The connection between process and landform is shown with sliders, animations, and changing shapes so you can see cause and effect instead of just reading about it.

Controls

Choose a scene, adjust parameters, and run the simulation to see the process produce the feature.

Made for the eeps art project — interactive educational demo.
